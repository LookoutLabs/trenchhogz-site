Situation Report

The war is small, round and pink

Squad-versus-squad turn-based artillery war in a flat-shaded PS1 world: aim it yourself, arc it over the parapet, and accept that the wind, the trench and your own mortar are all technically neutral parties.

Six classes, one very bad idea each

Grunt, Assault, Grenadier, Heavy, Medic, Sniper — plus the cloak-and-blade Phantom, the Dogs, and the Tribals with the only true arcs in the game.

A ten-weapon table and the dirty tricks

Rifles that fly flat inside the sights, bear traps that hold a grudge, C4, molotovs, and a bazooka answer to most architectural questions.

Killstreaks up to the end of the match

UAV, mortar racks, a steered missile flown from a field tablet, and a nuke with a five-beat cinematic and a bunker door that does not reopen.

Operations: the campaign as a ladder

Nine-rung towers with one roster and real permadeath — your pigs carry service records, and the memorial board remembers everyone who didn't.

The Steel Sow

A raid on a walking factory. Three systems, one pilot, one very large problem. Bring the squad; leave with an anecdote.

Countdown

One side arms the warhead, one side stops it — or gets behind the door. Five endings, all of them somebody's fault.

A home between tours

A walkable hangar hub, an outfitters, squad presets, daily contracts, ranks, medals, and a Play of the Match.

The look is a decision

One directional light, no PBR, dithered skies, optional CRT-Full glass. 1998 called; the war answered.

Permadeath, With Paperwork

The memorial board remembers

Operations are nine-rung ladders fought with one roster. Every pig carries a name, a class and a service record — and when one doesn't come back, the board in the hangar carries the rest.

"Bosun Broadside — Heavy, Blue Boars." You will rename him something shorter. You will regret how attached you got. The War Office thanks you for your continued enlistment.

The alpha trailer shot kit is in the can

Every beat of the ~70-second cut staged and captured — ProRes at 60fps, with a CRT-glass variant of every take, three maps of hands-off Director b-roll, and a contact sheet so nobody has to scrub. Every shot is re-stageable from a committed shot book, forever. The edit is next.

Capsules, screenshots, achievement icons — all of it renders from the game itself via a staged hero moment frozen in data. Re-art the game, re-run the bake, and the store page is honest again. 31 achievements wired hooks-ready, from First Blood to The Warhead Was for Show.
